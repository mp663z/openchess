# Trademark Policy

The project name and logo are trademarks of the project owner. This policy
explains what you may and may not do with them. It does not change the
copyright license: the code remains AGPL-3.0-or-later, and trademark rights
are separate from copyright.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to the project by name in text: tutorials, reviews, comparisons,
  talks, "powered by" statements that are true. Nominative fair use is fine
  - you may name the thing you are describing.
- Redistribute unmodified copies from the official repository, including
  the name and logo, under the license terms.
- State compatibility honestly ("works with", "imports from") without
  implying endorsement.

## What requires written permission

- Using the name or logo as part of the name of your own product, service,
  company, or domain.
- Modified distributions that present themselves under the project name or
  logo. If you distribute a modified version, pick a clearly different name
  and make the modification obvious (the AGPL already requires source and
  license notices).
- Any use that suggests your fork or service is official, sponsored, or
  endorsed. Absent written permission, state plainly on modified
  distributions: "not endorsed by the project owner".

## Logo use

The logo may be used unmodified to link to or identify the official
project. Modified logos, logo mashups, and logo use on merchandise require
written permission from the owner.

## Enforcement and questions

Trademark questions and permission requests go to the contact published in
the repository. The owner may revoke permission for uses that confuse users
about the origin of software or services.
