# Preregistration: H0 (T2289)

Registered 2026-09-18, before any data collection. Machine-readable form:
`validation/prereg-h0.yaml`. Original text immutable; amendments are appended
there with dates.

**H0.** Among weekly-playing 2000-2200 players, at least 40% have an importable
machine-readable repertoire (classes: maintained, scattered, course-bound are
importable; head-only and absent are not).

**Instrument.** Twenty artifact-led conversations: "show me your repertoire,"
then classify the artifact. Recruitment from clubs and online communities.
H0 precedes H1; the two fork cohorts stay separate.

**Decision rule.** >=40% importable keeps repertoire MAINTENANCE as the first
job; <40% forks to BUILDING (build-and-train). On the casual BUILDING path no
repertoire file is required: connected game history suffices to infer a de
facto repertoire and recurring errors.

**Execution note.** The conversations require 20 target users (resource per
T2289); recruitment is a human step outside this repo. This registration
freezes the hypothesis, rubric and decision rule so the measurement cannot be
fitted after the fact.
