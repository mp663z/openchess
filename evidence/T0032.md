# T0032 Install dependency license check - evidence

Acceptance: dependency license - seeded good case passes and seeded violation fails, naming task and SHA.

Implementation: check_dependency_license.py wraps the real tools/license_audit.py - which now parses SPDX expressions properly: OR of ANDs of bare license ids with exact allowlist token matching, fail closed on parentheses, WITH exceptions, leading/trailing operators (token-level guard: 'MIT OR GPL-2.0-only OR' rejected) and unknown tokens (closes the substring bypass where "MIT AND GPL-2.0-only" passed because it contains "MIT"). PEP 639 precedence: License-Expression is AUTHORITATIVE when present - a disallowed or malformed expression fails closed and cannot be rescued by a permissive legacy License field or classifier; legacy metadata is consulted only when no expression exists. Good mode: real audit over the repo's installed requirements is clean AND seeded good cases pass (GPL-2.0-only OR MIT - OR with one allowed branch; free-text "MIT License" field). Violation mode: FIVE seeded cases, each audited through the real audit in a FRESH subprocess (importlib.metadata caches make in-process injection unreliable - verified empirically): plain GPL-2.0-only, MIT AND GPL-2.0-only (the historical bypass), free text containing the token MIT, UNKNOWN, GPL-2.0-only OR CC-BY-NC-4.0 (OR with no allowed branch), GPL-2.0-only OR MIT OR (trailing operator), and three precedence attacks (disallowed/malformed License-Expression paired with a permissive legacy License field or classifier). Seeded good cases include the reverse precedence: an authoritative MIT expression overriding a GPL legacy field. Every case must be rejected; a crashed audit subprocess is a CheckError, never a silent pass.
Tests: tests/test_install_checks.py (dual-mode, per-case real-audit assertions for all seeded violation and good cases) + tests/test_license_audit.py (SPDX-subset truth table: 6 allowed / 12 rejected expression shapes, free-text rejection, trailing-License-word tolerance).
