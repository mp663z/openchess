# T3761 Revenue target contract - evidence

Acceptance: target is 1,000 active paying users during 2027 at EUR8 or USD10 monthly; currency/tax/refund/net-revenue definitions and target date are explicit.

Contract: data/contracts/revenue-target.yaml, values verbatim from product report v5 (sections 1, 10, Appendix C):
- 1,000 active paying users measured at 2027-12-31T23:59:59Z (good standing + successful charge in trailing 35 days).
- EUR 8 / USD 10 monthly; reporting currency EUR; USD converts at the ECB reference rate on the charge date.
- EU consumer prices VAT-inclusive; collected VAT/GST is remitted and never revenue.
- 14-day no-questions refunds, netted from net revenue in the month issued.
- net_revenue = gross_charges - refunds - taxes_remitted - payment_processing_fees; reference: 1000 x EUR 8 x 12 = EUR 96,000 ARR gross (~EUR96k in v5); ~90% margin guardrail applies to net.
- Funnel derived backwards: 100,000 visitors -> 5,000 active free (5%) -> 1,000 paying (20%); re-derived monthly once instruments report.
