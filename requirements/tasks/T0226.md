# T0226 Store/backup/fuzz/fault

## Normative requirements

1. The battery is deterministic and seeded. It fuzzes the shipped production backup (`store.backup`, T0224). Every input log is built from an independent model of data/contracts/wal.yaml and data/contracts/backup.yaml: the entry-id derivation, the put/delete fold, the bundle serialization and the backup-id derivation are restated in the test, never taken from production. `graph.diff.state_id` supplies state ids, and the contract reference engine (`tests.test_t0221_backup_contract`) is the differential oracle for every case.
2. The seven generators produce 614 cases:
   - `happy` (64): logs of 0-24 entries back up to the model receipt, with exactly one serializer call on the model state.
   - `accepted` (16): any exact UTF-8 str from the serializer becomes the bundle and is bound into the id.
   - `source` (150): 25 single-entry tampers at the first, middle and last entry. They include sequence edits (with self-consistent re-chained forgeries, true and int subclass), unknown and str-subclass ops, id and prior flips, a trailing-newline id, extra keys, same-arity renames at the entry, payload and record levels, str-subclass and hash-colliding keys, digest, identity and payload swaps, a str-subclass identity, dict-subclass records and entries, None or list entries, and duplicates. Each fails as `corrupt_source` before the serializer is called.
   - `container` (6): non-list and list-subclass sources are `malformed_backup_record`.
   - `serializer` (63): a forged `BackupError` of every class, a forged `WalError` of every class, six BaseException kinds and six bad output types, on an empty and a non-empty log.
   - `live` (72): a serializer edits the caller's live log (keys added at every level, values changed, fields dropped, records and entries cleared, container append, insert, delete, reverse and clear), then answers honestly, raises or returns bytes.
   - `verify` (243): 81 receipt tampers, pinned as `malformed_backup_record`, `divergent_backup` or accepted. They include shape, dict subclass, same-arity renames, str-subclass and colliding keys per field, count types and bounds (true, float, str, int subclass, -1, max+1, 2*max, huge, max accepted when re-derived), id grammar (str subclass, non-str, newline, leading space, upper case, short, long), bundle type, str subclass and surrogate, flips, consistency forgeries, and accepted self-consistent re-derivations and reordered input.
3. Every case holds against production. There is no raw escape: failures are only a typed `BackupError` with its mapped code. The outcome is the independently known one and the same as the contract reference. At the serializer boundary the error is FRESH, never the forged object. The log (or receipt) is identical afterwards by value, exact type, key order and object identity at every level. verify never calls the serializer and returns a detached, typed copy in contract order.
4. Outcomes are deterministic by value, and rejections do not poison a later backup or verify. The count bound is checked from both sides.
5. Mutation check: each of the 37 one-guard source mutants of store/backup.py fails the corpus, with `BackupError` and `_fail` rebound to the production class.
   - Boundary mutants: except Exception, BackupError pass-through for all classes and for each class, and WalError pass-through for all classes and for each class. They are pinned in `FORGE_TARGETS` to their exact forged-case label and must fail exactly that case.
   - Source mutants: isinstance, missing or narrowed checks, and no log restore.
   - Verify mutants: key guard, key length or superset, isinstance receipt, id, count or bundle, `match`/`search` for grammar, count max, exclusive max or min, the zero-count checks, the genesis check, the backup-id check, the UTF-8 check in derivation, and verify returning the input or input order.
   - The identity mutant is green. The one equivalent edit (no UTF-8 check on serializer output, subsumed by the derivation check with the same class) is asserted green.

## Requirement-to-test mapping

- R1, R2, R3: `test_fuzz_cases_hold` (one per generator), `test_corpus_reaches_every_class_and_position`, `test_every_boundary_error_class_is_forged`.
- R4: `test_determinism_by_value`, `test_no_poisoning_after_rejections`, `test_count_bound_from_both_sides`.
- R5: `test_identity_source_mutant_is_green`, `test_mutant_is_red`, `test_equivalent_edits_stay_green`.
