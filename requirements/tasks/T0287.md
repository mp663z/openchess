# T0287 Jobs/queue/implement

## Normative requirements

1. `server.jobs_queue` implements `QueueError`, `QueueEngine` with `apply(state, request)`, `job_id_for`, `state_id_for` and `FAILURE_MAPPING` for data/contracts/queue.yaml (contract id `jobs-queue`). Production must not import or execute `tests.*`.
2. The request is checked first (`malformed_queue_request`), then the state (`corrupt_queue`): exact types only, dict keys checked as exact strs before any set or hash comparison, identifiers by ASCII fullmatch, ints bounded (priority 0..9, now 0..2**53-1, lease_ms 1..3600000 with now + lease_ms within the clock limit), payloads walked iteratively and without raising (exact built-in JSON, UTF-8 strs, ints of at most MAX_INT_DIGITS digits, finite floats, depth at most MAX_DEPTH, no container met twice across all payloads of a state), seqs strictly increasing below next_seq, unique job ids, and the status/lease/attempts invariants.
3. Transitions run on a detached canonical copy and commit in place last, so a rejected call leaves state and request unchanged: enqueue is idempotent by dedupe key (`dedupe_conflict` on a different priority or payload, `capacity_exceeded` past max_jobs or the seq space); claim leases the claimable job with the lowest (priority, seq) and dead-marks claimable jobs already at max_attempts on the way; ack/nack only by the lease holder strictly before expiry (`lease_conflict`, `unknown_job`). Errors are fresh typed QueueErrors with no cause or context.
4. The T0286 red battery turns green by replacing only its two binding lines. Every row, probe and mutant stays unchanged.
