# T0297 Jobs/idempotency/unit+property

## Normative requirements

1. Unit decisions of `server.jobs_idempotency.JobsIdempotencyEngine`: atomic commit of transition and receipt together, replay returning an equal FRESH receipt committing nothing, key-then-fingerprint conflict (`key_conflict`), chain growth (sequence, prior/state ids), stale-composition rollback (`corrupt_ledger`), unwrapped propagated queue rejections (exact QueueError, no phantom receipt, retry re-attempts), validation order request then state then ledger, oracle call-count zero on invalid inputs, BaseException-raising and closure-mutating oracles failing closed with inputs restored, request/state graph-disjointness, result/stored detachment, caller-request identity and value preservation, by-value determinism, key-grammar edges, and the linked queue contract's payload int-digit limit.
2. Seeded properties against an independent model that restates both contracts' rules and shares no code with the engine beyond the public id helpers (`job_id_for`, `state_id_for`, `request_fingerprint`, `derive_receipt_id`) and the shared contract limit constants (`MAX_JOBS`, `MAX_ATTEMPTS`): per-step agreement on error layer (idempotency vs propagated queue) and failure class, and per success on the outcome word and receipt, with state and ledger compared under the canonical (type-distinguishing) JSON serialization; stream replay determinism; ledger chain invariants after every step; hostile (randomly SystemExit-raising) oracles failing closed as `divergent_fingerprint` committing nothing once validation passed. Scope: the streams are finite (150 steps, 6 dedupe keys), so capacity/seq-exhaustion domains are not modeled; the broader hostile-input, forged-oracle and closure taxonomies remain owned by the T0295 battery bound to production. A named hand-built regression surrogate (FlipOutcome, flips the outcome word on success) is proven caught by the property (735 wrong outcomes pinned for the surrogate as defined in the file).

## Requirement-to-test mapping

- R1: the sixteen `test_*` unit cases in `tests/test_t0297_idempotency_unit_property.py`.
- R2: `test_engine_matches_independent_model_on_random_streams` (40 seeds x 150 steps), `test_replaying_the_same_stream_is_deterministic` (10 seeds), `test_ledger_chain_invariants_hold_after_every_step` (20 seeds), `test_hostile_oracle_streams_fail_closed_and_never_commit` (10 seeds).
