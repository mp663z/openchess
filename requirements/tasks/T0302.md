# T0302 Jobs/cancel/contract

## Normative requirements

1. data/contracts/cancel.yaml is the normative contract. tools/cancel_contract_lint.py pins it exactly and binds max_attempts, the job_id grammar, the worker (requester) grammar, job_fields, the statuses and the priority and payload bounds to data/contracts/queue.yaml with exact full literals (a prefix-preserving sibling drift fails).
2. `cancel {op, job, requester, now}` cancels only a job that satisfies every queue job invariant and has status ready or leased. Precedence: request shape (malformed_cancel_request), then job integrity (corrupt_job), then cancellable status (job_not_cancellable).
3. The record is exactly `{op, job_id, seq, priority, payload_digest, status_before, attempts, requester, cancelled_at, decision_id}`. payload_digest and decision_id use the exact pd1/cd1 preimages stated in the contract. The payload itself is never copied. Lease fields are never copied either: the record carries status_before and attempts only.
4. Every rejection is a fresh typed error with retryable false. No user code runs on hostile input, and the input is left unchanged.
5. Fail-closed readings, stated in the contract rule text: a ready job has not started and cancellation withdraws it before any claim; a leased job is claimed and the decision is recorded against the live lease; the presented status governs (lease expiry and reclaim are the queue contract's concern, never recomputed here); a recorded cancellation is terminal and never reversed by this contract.

## Not covered (flagged)

Applying the decision to queue state, worker abort, deduplication of repeated cancellations (the T0293 idempotency contract), and redrive of a cancelled job. These are parked for the owner, matching the parked notes in the retry, progress, limit, dead-letter and restart sibling contracts.
